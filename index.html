Common Questions

Payer Source FAQs

Does Medicare cover all home health services?
Medicare covers skilled home health services when you are homebound and your doctor orders the care — typically at no cost to you. This includes skilled nursing, therapy, and home health aide services.
Do you accept Superior and Molina plans?
Yes. We are contracted with both Superior Health Plan and Molina Healthcare for Texas Medicaid, including STAR and STAR+PLUS managed care plans.
Can veterans use TriWest for home health?
Yes. We accept TriWest Healthcare Alliance through the VA Community Care Network (CCN), allowing eligible veterans to receive home health services in their area.
Will you bill my insurance directly?
Yes. We handle all billing on your behalf for every insurance plan we accept. You won't need to file claims yourself — our billing specialists take care of everything.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most from patients and families.

What is home health care?
Home health care is medical and supportive care provided in a patient's home. It includes skilled nursing, physical therapy, occupational therapy, speech therapy, personal care, and more — delivered by licensed professionals.
Who qualifies for home health care?
Patients who are homebound, have a qualifying medical condition, and have a physician's order for home health services typically qualify. Our team can help verify your eligibility.
How do I start home health services?
Call us at (972) 479-1500, submit our online admission form, or have your physician refer you directly. We'll verify your insurance, create a care plan, and schedule your first visit — usually within 24–48 hours.
Is home health care covered by insurance?
Yes, in most cases. Medicare, Medicaid, and most private insurance plans cover qualifying home health services. Our billing team will verify your coverage at no charge before services begin.
How are your caregivers vetted?
All Reliant First staff undergo comprehensive background checks, license verification, drug screening, and competency testing before being assigned to any patient.

We collect several types of information to provide and improve our services:

  • Personal Identification: Name, address, phone number, email, and date of birth.
  • Health Information: Medical history, diagnoses, treatment records, medications, and insurance information.
  • Insurance Information: Carrier, member ID, group number, and coverage details.
  • Website Usage: IP address, browser type, pages visited (via standard web analytics).
  • Communications: Messages, emails, or correspondence you send to us.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to provide, coordinate, and manage home health care services; process insurance claims and manage billing; communicate with you about your care; comply with legal and regulatory requirements; improve our website and services; and respond to your inquiries.

Information Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only with healthcare providers involved in your care, your insurance carrier for billing, when required by law, or with your explicit written consent.

HIPAA Compliance

As a home health agency, Reliant First Healthcare Services is a covered entity under HIPAA. We are fully committed to compliance with all HIPAA Privacy and Security Rules. Your Protected Health Information (PHI) is used and disclosed only as permitted by HIPAA. All staff receive regular HIPAA training and are required to maintain the confidentiality of patient information.

Data Security

We implement a variety of security measures including encryption of sensitive data, secure password-protected systems with role-based access controls, regular security assessments, physical security measures, and prompt notification procedures in the event of a data breach.

Your Rights

You have the right to access your health records, request corrections to inaccurate information, request restrictions on certain uses or disclosures, request confidential communications, receive breach notifications, and file a complaint with us or the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services.
